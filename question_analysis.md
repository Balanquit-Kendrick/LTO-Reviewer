1. Question: What should you do in case your vehicle breaks down on an expressway? Check all that apply.
   Given Answer: [0, 2, 3, 4] (Open trunk/hood, Call for help, Park far right, Put hazard lights)
   Status: Correct
   Explanation: These are standard emergency procedures. Opening hood/trunk signals distress, calling for help is essential, parking far right ensures safety, and hazard lights warn other drivers.

2. Question: What will happen when your front tire blows out?
   Given Answer: [2] (The front end will pull towards the side of the blowout)
   Status: Correct
   Explanation: Physics dictates that a blown front tire will cause the vehicle to pull toward the side with the flat tire due to loss of grip and traction.

   Given Answer: [3] (Pull over to the right and slow down or even stop if necessary)
   Status: Correct
   Explanation: This is the standard emergency vehicle protocol under Philippine traffic laws - yield right of way by moving right and stopping if needed.
